# Determination of squid colonization levels using spiral plating

This note provides instructions for using the Neutec EddyJet and SphereFlash for the determination of colonization levels. 
Colonization experiments should be performed per lab protocol found [here](../files/squid-colonization.md) with the modificaitons to dilution prep specified below. Dilutions can then be plated and counted using the EddyJet and SphereFlash.

## Modifications to Squid Colonization Protocol

### Step 14.14 - Changing the volume of the water with the squid

Orignal Protocol: 
> Transfer each squid in a volume of approximately 700 μl to a 1.5 ml microcentrifuge tube from Step 4.12. Move to a cardboard freezer box. Once the lid is placed on the box, do not remove it as the light cues for bacteria expulsion are not well-understood.

Instead of 700 uL, fill the tube to 1 mL. The machine calculates CFU/mL by default, but this would not be synonomous with CFU/mL if the sample comes from a 700 uL source. 

If you do not make this modification, you will need to do ONE of the following to obtain correct counts.
- When squid are thawed for plating, top off the microtubes to 1 mL using FSIO (step 5.7 in colonization protocol). After plating/counting, use the CFU/mL value from the SphereFlash as reported
- If you use the 700uL samples for dilutions and plating, after getting the CFU/mL value from the SphereFlash you will need to calculate how many bacteria would be in 700 uL given the CFU/mL concentration it calculated for you. Take the CFU/mL reported and multiply it by 0.7. 

### Section 5 - Changing how dilutions are made

The preparation of squid dilutions is done similarly to the original protocol with the following exceptions:
 - Only the "B" dilution needs to be made (5.1, 5.12)
 - There should be 1000 uL final volume instead of 500 uL final volume for the dilution (5.1, 5.12)
 - The "A" dilution has a volume of 1 mL to start (5.7, 5.11).

Revised Section 5 of the [Colonization Protocol](../files/squid-colonization.md):
1. For each squid, prepare one (1) microcentrifuge tube, each with 950 μl FSIO (or autoclaved 70% Instant Ocean).
1. Prepare pestles by first using a Kimwipe to clean the pestle and remove gross debris and/or tissue.
1. Place pestles tip-down in a 50 ml beaker containing 95% ethanol. Ethanol should be added to a height of approximately 3 cm.
1. For each pestle, remove from the beaker and wipe the tip with a Kimwipe.
1. Dip pestle back into the ethanol bath, remove and insert (tip up) into an microcentrifuge tube rack and allow to air dry completely for approximately 15 minutes.
1. Thaw squid in a microcentrifuge tube rack (maximum n=8).
1. If necessary, adjust the volume to 1 mL.
1. Using a pestle from Step 5.5, disrupt the animal tissue until the ink sac ruptures (the water will turn a murky grey color).
1. Remove the pestle and ensure all tissue remains in the tube.
1. Vortex the tissue briefly for exactly 10 seconds (use a timer).
1. Allow the tissue to rest for 10 min. The tissue will settle and the bacteria and ink remain in solution For the calculations that follow, the bacteria/ink solution is the [A] dilution (i.e. the *E. scolopes* light organ homogenate in 1 mL). Serial 1:20 dilutions [B] is described below.
1. For the [B] dilution, add 50 μl [A] to one of the microcentrifuge tubes prepared in Step 5.1. Vortex.
1. Plate using the Eddy Jet sprial plater
